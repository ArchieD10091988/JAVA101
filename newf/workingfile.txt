1. The 2 daily dataset : BPP.IMX.RL63.BTI.LOANPRIN.MMMD1YY & BPP.IMX.RL63.BTI.LOANINTR.MMMD1YY are currently the input dataset to form
the monthly PSLOAN.

2. The said 2 daily dataset are a daily accumulation of records from first day of the month up to the last. 

3. To form the 'Daily PSLOAN', we just need to consider the below : 

- Merge the 2 daily dataset and filter the transaction date field : PSLOAN-TRANDAT with date format (YYMMDD) equal to the process date / batch date 
- Once merge, remove the first 2 character of field : PSLOAN-DEAL-NO
